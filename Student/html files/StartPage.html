How it works

From application to evaluation in 4 steps

One platform for the whole internship, so nothing gets lost between the student, the company and the university.

1

Create your profile

Register with your student ID, add your skills and upload the required documents.

2

Find & apply

Search internships, see how well your skills match, save favourites and apply in one click.

3

Accept your offer

Read the offer letter, accept it, and your coordinator reviews and approves the placement.

4

Report & get evaluated

Submit weekly progress reports and receive feedback until your final evaluation.

Features

Everything a student needs

Built around the real internship process of our university.

🔍

Smart internship search

Filter by department, location, duration and work mode, and sort by best skill match.

📄

Digital offer letters

View, accept or decline offers and print the letter as a PDF whenever you need it.

📁

Document checklist

Drag and drop your CV, transcript and ID, and see exactly which documents are still missing.

📝

Weekly progress reports

Log hours and achievements each week and track them in a simple chart.

✅

Coordinator approval

Follow the status of your placement and read your coordinator's feedback in one place.

🔔

Instant notifications

Get notified about offers, approvals and deadlines so you never miss a step.
